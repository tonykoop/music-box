# Music Box Drawing Brief

No dimensioned drawing is authorized yet. This is an `L2 V5 build-packet
candidate` — decision-record.md explicitly rejects CAD/DXF/fabrication
drawing release until comb, pin-release, registration, and speed-control
mules produce measured evidence (design.md "Acceptance Evidence For L3
Consideration").

`cad/music-box.scad` is a `pending_measurement` envelope scaffold (comb tooth
geometry from wolfram/music-box-wolfram-model.wl estimates; cylinder length
derived from tooth-lane count; cylinder diameter is an explicit non-dimension
placeholder). It is not a drawing and must not be treated as one.

## Required first drawings (future, gated)

- Comb coupon drawing: measured tooth length/width/thickness once a coupon
  passes VAL-001.
- Pin-release detail: measured pin profile/lift/clearance once VAL-002 passes.
- Cylinder registration drawing: measured lane pitch and datum scheme once
  VAL-003 passes.
- Governor/escapement assembly drawing: once VAL-004 passes.

## Authority Notes

- No cylinder diameter, pin grid, comb tooth-length schedule, or governor
  dimension exists anywhere in this packet. Any future drawing must trace to
  measured coupon/mule data, not this concept packet or the Wolfram estimate
  model.
